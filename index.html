Character Spotlight

My Favorite Character

Bottom Drama Performance

My favorite character is Bottom, the weaver. He’s so funny and full of energy. When Quince cast Bottom as Pyramus, Bottom didn’t seem happy about it. He wanted to play Thisbe, the Lion, and even the entire cast all by himself, and he boasted, “I’ll raise a storm!” It’s that confidence that makes him so funny. The actor’s performance was truly extraordinary. His voice was expressive and funny, his movements were nimble and energetic, and his timing was spot-on, so the audience laughed at his jokes.

Iconic Moment

My Favorite Scene

Bottom and Titania (Bottom wakes up Titania) Act 3 / Scene 1

One of my favorite scenes from the play is when Puck transforms Bottom’s appearance and he wakes Titania from her slumber with his singing. The scene is very funny and Titania falls instantly in love with him declaring him wise and handsome. Bottom replies to her declaration with the witty line “Common sense and love rarely go hand in hand these". The costume Bottom wore during that scene was a monkey mask, which looked very funny. His energetic and humorous performance was perfectly in tune with the storyline, making the audience laugh.

Social Media

Social Media

Instagram

Instagram is a social media app owned by Meta where people share photos, Reels, and Stories with friends and followers.

Strengths
  • ✓ Stories and Close Friends make it easy to share everyday moments without everyone seeing them
  • ✓ Photos and videos look clean, so it’s a good place for personal branding
Weaknesses
  • ✕ The video algorithm is too fast, and strange videos often appear
  • ✕ A lot of ads that are very distracting
Social Media

TikTok

TikTok is a short-video app from ByteDance where users watch, make, and share videos, often with music, effects, and trends.

Strengths
  • ✓ The For You Page (FYP) is very good at finding videos I like, even when I don’t follow the creator
  • ✓ The built-in editing tools, sounds, and effects make it easy to create a video without any extra app
Weaknesses
  • ✕ It’s very addictive, and I often end up scrolling for much longer than I planned
  • ✕ Some content is low quality or just copies a trend, and misinformation spreads quickly too
  • ✕ There are too many ads popping up, and they're really annoying
